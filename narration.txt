Demo Narration Script

Opening screen
1. “Every food-safety incident starts small. One packet, one complaint, one very uncomfortable question: how far did it travel?”

Factory scene, F1
2. “Here’s the source. On Line 4, the sealing temperature drops from 160 to 134 degrees for 18 minutes. Not ideal. The batch still passes through without a quality hold, because apparently this carton chose chaos.”

Distribution scene, F2
3.“Those affected cartons are dispatched and delivered across 40 FreshMart stores. At this point, the issue is no longer sitting neatly in a factory. It is out in the wild.”

Customer discovery, F3
4.“Then Sarah finds mould in a packet. This is the moment a production defect becomes a customer incident.”
Social complaint

5.“Sarah posts about it. The complaint gains attention, and now the business needs answers quickly, clearly, and with evidence.”

Human coordination
6. “This is how the response often begins: QA asks Plant. Plant asks Supply Chain. Supply Chain needs access. Analytics gets a spreadsheet with creative column names.”
7. “Four hours and 32 minutes later, the batch is confirmed, but the root cause and full store exposure are still unresolved. Everyone is working. The answer is not.”

Transition to agents
8.“Now, let’s give the same incident to Intugle.”

Agent introduction
9.“Three agents start from the same customer complaint, but instead of passing context from team to team, they connect the evidence directly.”

Unstructured Agent
10.“First, the Unstructured Agent turns Sarah’s image and complaint into usable incident evidence. It identifies the affected batch and links it to production data.”

Analysis Agent
11.“Next, the Analysis Agent connects quality, warehouse, and sales records. It finds the temperature deviation, confirms the release path, and traces the exposure to 40 stores.”

Approval gate
12.“The Resolution Agent needs read-only access to compliance rules. Intugle does not quietly help itself to restricted information. Sensible.”

Diagnosis
13. “Now we have an evidence-backed diagnosis: batch HLD-2407-A, an 18-minute sealing-temperature deviation, and exposure across 40 stores.”

Decision console
14.“Intugle presents response options. The recommendation is to withdraw the affected stock and publish a clear customer statement. The system prepares the work; a person approves the response.”

Execution
15.“Once approved, response agents execute in sequence. One notifies stores and withdraws stock. One prepares and publishes the statement. One resolves the customer case and refund.”

Outcome
16.“In approximately 47 simulated seconds, the business moves from a viral complaint to a governed, evidence-backed response.”

Customer update
17.“And Sarah gets the update that matters: the issue was taken seriously, the right action was taken, and someone actually followed through.”

Close
18.“Intugle helps teams move from incident noise to connected evidence, responsible decisions, and action. Faster, without skipping the human judgment.”
